# 🛡️ UPI Safety Coach

### Your Smart Assistant for Detecting UPI Scams

UPI Safety Coach is a chatbot prototype designed to help users identify suspicious Unified Payments Interface (UPI) payment situations. It explains potential scam warning signs, provides a risk assessment, and suggests practical steps to stay safe while making digital payments.

## 🎯 Objective

To raise awareness about UPI-related fraud and help people make safer digital payment decisions through an easy-to-use AI-powered chatbot.

## ✨ Features

- **Scam Risk Assessment:** Analyzes suspicious payment scenarios and identifies potential fraud indicators.
- **Clear Explanations:** Explains why a situation may be suspicious.
- **Safety Recommendations:** Suggests practical steps users can take to protect themselves.
- **UPI Fraud Awareness:** Helps users recognize common digital payment scams.
- **User-Friendly Interface:** Allows users to describe a situation in everyday language.
- **Financial Safety Education:** Encourages users to verify transactions and avoid sharing confidential banking information.

## 🚨 Scams the Project Can Help Identify

Examples of scenarios the chatbot can be designed to assess:

- Fake UPI payment screenshots
- QR-code payment scams
- Requests to enter a UPI PIN to receive money
- Fake customer-care calls
- Phishing links and fraudulent messages
- Fake cashback and reward offers
- Impersonation scams
- Requests to share OTPs or other confidential information
- Fake payment confirmation messages
- Suspicious collect requests

The chatbot's assessment is advisory and should not be treated as a guarantee that a transaction is safe or fraudulent.

## 🔐 Security Guidelines

- Never share your UPI PIN, ATM PIN, password, or OTP.
- Never enter your UPI PIN merely to receive money.
- Verify received payments in your official banking or UPI app.
- Do not trust screenshots as proof of payment.
- Do not click suspicious payment links.
- Contact your bank through its official channels if you suspect fraud.
- Never publish API keys, passwords, or private credentials in a public repository.

## 🌐 Deployment

The frontend may be deployed using a static hosting service such as GitHub Pages, provided it is configured for static deployment.

If the application requires a Node.js/Express backend or Gemini API calls through `server.ts`, that backend must be hosted separately on a compatible service. GitHub Pages cannot run the backend directly.

## ⚠️ Disclaimer

UPI Safety Coach is an educational prototype intended to promote digital payment awareness. Its results may be inaccurate or incomplete and must not be treated as professional financial advice or a guarantee of transaction safety. Always verify payment details through official banking channels.

If fraud is suspected in India, contact your bank promptly and report cyber financial fraud through the official helpline **1930** or the [National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal](https://cybercrime.gov.in/).
